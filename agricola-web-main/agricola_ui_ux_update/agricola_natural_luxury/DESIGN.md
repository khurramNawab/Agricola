## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "Verdant Purity"—a high-end, organic agricultural ethos crafted specifically for the modern Indian premium market. It bridges ancestral agrarian authenticity with frictionless, contemporary luxury commerce. The aesthetic targets discerning consumers seeking certified organic seeds, pure GI-tagged Bihar makhana (foxnut), single-origin Malabar spices, and artisanal wellness staples.

The visual style merges **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Refined Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- Earth-rich warmth replaces clinical e-commerce minimalism.
- Transparent, glazed layers and muted borders evoke sunlit greenhouse panes and clean dew droplets.
- Deep botanical contrast guarantees high readability, trustworthiness, and prestige.
- The interface feels grounded, abundant, sensory, and meticulously curated.

## Colors

The palette transforms raw botanical vitality into an opulent, structured hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#84b817` - Vibrant Leaf)**: The core action anchor. Represents new life, vitality, and organic harvest.
  - Hover state: `#6d9913`
  - Active state: `#5c8210`
  - Subtle tint surface: `#f4f8ec`, `#eaf3db`, `#e2eed0`
- **Secondary (`#1e3a1f` - Deep Canopy Forest)**: Grounded structural authority used for primary navigation bars, headings, high-emphasis buttons, and deep visual contrast.
  - Mid-tone variant: `#2d5a27` (subheaders, badges, active tab markers).
- **Tertiary Accent (`#d97706` - Warm Harvest Amber & Gold)**: Evoking turmeric, mustard blooms, saffron, and premium harvest certifications.
  - Gold Accent: `#f59e0b` (ratings, review stars, authenticity crests).
  - Amber Tint Wash: `#fef3c7` (badge containers, notice backdrops).
- **Neutrals (Warm Soil & Crushed Grain)**: Replaces stark digital white with comforting natural surfaces.
  - Base Background: `#faf8f5` (warm cream silk)
  - Surface Neutral: `#f5f2eb`
  - Elevated Container / Border: `#eae5dc`
  - Body Slate Dark: `#303e31`
  - Primary Text / Deep Slate: `#1a241b`
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Success: `#16a34a` (tint: `#dcfce7`)
  - Warning: `#d97706` (tint: `#fef3c7`)
  - Error: `#dc2626` (tint: `#fee2e2`)
  - Info: `#0284c7` (tint: `#e0f2fe`)

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers the design system across all responsive viewports. Its geometric skeleton paired with subtle humanist curved terminals creates an inviting, premium retail presence while preserving exceptional numerical legibility for Rupee pricing (`₹`), batch numbers, and nutritional metrics.

- **Weight Pairings**: Heavy display titles (`700` and `800`) are balanced with airy line-heights on body copy (`400`) to evoke spaciousness.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Pair large titles in `#1e3a1f` (Forest Green) with muted secondary labels in `#303e31` (Deep Slate) to ensure instant hierarchy.
- **Strict Compliance**: Body text meets a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against natural off-white backgrounds; headers achieve a minimum of 7:1.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a fluid, human-centric 8pt baseline rhythm bounded within a maximum container width of `80rem` (1280px) for standard viewports, with an extended cinematic container of `90rem` (1440px) for immersive visual storytelling.

- **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` outer gutters and `0.75rem` inner column gutters. Cards stack vertically or scroll along a snap-aligned horizontal axis with `0.5rem` peek reveals.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Product grids default to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic auto margins. E-commerce catalogues utilize a 4-column item layout with a fixed 3-column sticky sidebar for filters and harvest provenance indicators.
- **Rhythm Principle**: Internal card padding uses generous spacing (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to prevent dense marketplace clutter and reinforce the luxury retail experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent frosted planes layered over warm, organic backdrops rather than sharp, dark drop-shadows.

1. **Subtle Tinted Shadows**:
   Instead of neutral black shadows, depth is anchored using a botanical forest-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -8px rgba(30, 58, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -4px rgba(30, 58, 31, 0.04)`. This mimics natural sunlight diffused through greenery.
2. **Glassmorphic Tiers**:
   - **Base (Tier 0)**: Background `#faf8f5` canvas.
   - **Floating Panel (Tier 1)**: `bg-[#faf8f5]/85 backdrop-blur-md border border-[#1e3a1f]/10 shadow-sm`.
   - **Interactive Card (Tier 2)**: `bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-lg border border-white/60 shadow-[0_12px_30px_-8px_rgba(30,58,31,0.08)]`. On hover, the element lifts: `-translate-y-1 shadow-[0_20px_40px_-12px_rgba(30,58,31,0.12)]`.
   - **Modal / Overlay Sheet (Tier 3)**: Solid or translucent `#faf8f5` combined with an ambient scrim `rgba(26, 36, 27, 0.45) backdrop-blur-sm`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, organic geometries inspired by seeds, water droplets, and handcrafted ceramics:

- **Containers & Glass Cards**: Standardized on `1rem` (`rounded-2xl`, 16px) for compact widgets, expanding to `1.5rem` (`rounded-3xl`, 24px) for hero promotional banners, category tiles, and showcase product cards.
- **Interactive Buttons & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or deeply rounded (`rounded-xl`, 12px) to provide inviting touch targets for mobile thumb-zones.
- **Image Frames**: Outlined with soft borders (`border border-[#1e3a1f]/08`) using a minimum curvature of `16px` (`rounded-2xl`), eliminating sharp, harsh corners across the platform.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid brand green `#84b817` with bold white `#ffffff` text, `rounded-xl` or `rounded-full`, `px-6 py-3.5`, font weight `600`. Hover shifts smoothly to `#6d9913` with soft leaf shadow `shadow-[0_8px_20px_-4px_rgba(132,184,23,0.35)]`.
- **Secondary Action**: Deep canopy forest `#1e3a1f` with warm cream text `#faf8f5`, pairing high visual authority for checkout and bulk farmer transactions.
- **Ghost / Outline**: Transparent ground with border `border-[#1e3a1f]/20`, text `#1e3a1f`. On hover: `bg-[#f4f8ec] border-[#84b817] text-[#5c8210]`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Best Seller / GI Provenance Tag**: Warm harvest amber `bg-[#fef3c7] text-[#d97706] border border-[#f59e0b]/30 rounded-full px-3 py-1 font-semibold text-xs`.
- **Organic Certified Badges**: `bg-[#f4f8ec] text-[#2d5a27] border border-[#84b817]/25 rounded-full px-3 py-1 flex items-center gap-1.5`.
- **Filter Chips**: Inactive states feature `bg-white/70 border border-[#eae5dc] text-[#303e31] rounded-full`. Active states switch to `bg-[#1e3a1f] text-[#faf8f5] border-[#1e3a1f] shadow-sm`.

### Product & Story Cards
- **Frosted Glass Cards**: Built with `bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/60 rounded-3xl p-5 shadow-[0_12px_30px_-8px_rgba(30,58,31,0.08)] transition-all duration-300 hover:-translate-y-1.5`.
- **Image Container**: Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5, set inside `bg-[#f5f2eb] rounded-2xl overflow-hidden relative`.
- **Price Block**: Rupee currency indicator in `#1e3a1f` weight `700`, with strikethrough MRP in `#303e31`/50 and percentage discount highlighted in bold `#84b817`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: `bg-white/80 border border-[#eae5dc] rounded-xl px-4 py-3 text-[#1a241b] placeholder:text-[#303e31]/40 focus:border-[#84b817] focus:ring-2 focus:ring-[#84b817]/20 focus:outline-none transition-all`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular or soft-square widgets accented in `#84b817` with subtle inner drop checkmarks in white; unselected state is framed in `#eae5dc` over `#faf8f5`.

- **Soil & Harvest Freshness Meter**: A segmented visual indicator showing batch harvest date and moisture integrity with gradient fills from `#84b817` to `#f59e0b`.
