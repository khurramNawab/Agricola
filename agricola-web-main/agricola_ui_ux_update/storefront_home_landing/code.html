Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our harvest origins, testing standards, and logistics.

AgriCola works exclusively with farms holding Jaivik Bharat (NPOP/APEDA) certifications. Furthermore, every batch brought to our fulfillment centers in Kaithal and Purnia undergoes sample testing at NABL-accredited labs for over 180 pesticide residues, synthetic growth accelerators, and heavy metals before packaging.

We operate two primary climate-monitored hubs: the Purnia Hub in Bihar (handling Mithila Phool Makhana, cold-pressed mustard oil, and eastern spices) and the Kaithal Hub in Haryana (handling organic seeds, ancient millets, and northern grains). This allows immediate dispatch without intermediary warehousing.

Our packaging utilizes multi-layer kraft barrier pouches and nitrogen-flushed food-grade canisters. This preserves natural volatile oils and shields against ambient Indian humidity. Shelf life is 9 to 12 months from packing date, with no artificial preservatives or sulfur additives used.

Every package is stamped with a unique QR code tied to our public traceability ledger. Scanning it on your phone displays the farm cluster geo-location, soil testing scorecard, grower cluster head photo, date of popping/roasting, and lab chemical assay.

We operate an unconditional 7-day "Purity & Crunch Guarantee". If you receive a damaged seal or find the texture less than exceptional, contact us via WhatsApp or email for a zero-friction replacement or full refund to your original payment mode.
